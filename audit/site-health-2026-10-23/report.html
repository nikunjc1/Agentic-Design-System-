Scope limits

Changes and verification apply to the local project; no deployment was made. Browser checks blocked external network requests, so externally hosted fonts were not verified. Prompt checks validate the exported instructions and current configuration; they do not establish the quality of a separate AI tool's generated implementation. The page sweep is a smoke check, supplemented by the workflows listed above.
