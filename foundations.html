Foundations · Color

Color

The primary, secondary and tertiary colors that drive every semantic token, component and generated design in this system.

Brand colors

Primary defines the system - every semantic color token derives from it. Secondary and tertiary are optional accents; leave them off if your brand doesn't use them. Whatever you pick, choose colors that hold up against WCAG A, AA and AAA contrast guidelines so text and interactive states stay readable and accessible for everyone.

Background colors

The neutral surface stack every panel, card and layer sits on - independent of your brand color. Primary is the base canvas the whole page sits on; Secondary sits one level above it (panels, cards); Tertiary sits above that (nested or hovered surfaces, popovers).
